This repository contains the official brand identity, logomark, and visual assets for **Linkest Foundation**. 

These assets are provided to ensure a consistent and accurate representation of our foundation across open-source projects, documentation, official partnerships, and promotional materials.

## 🎨 The "Fish-Bread" Concept

The Linkest logomark is a stylized, abstract geometric shape that evokes the imagery of a **fish** and a **bun/bread**. 

- **Color Palette:** A gradient of Cornflower Blue to Violet.
- **Inspiration:** Derived from the ancient Chinese philosophical text *Zhuangzi* ("North Sea has a fish, its name is Kun..."), representing boundless freedom and evolution. It is also a tribute to the independent creators who fuel the open-source ecosystem—often coding late into the night, surviving on bread.
- **The Shape:** The curves represent connection (Link) and fluidity, adapting seamlessly to both light and dark backgrounds.

## 📂 Directory Structure

- `/svg`: Vector formats (Scalable, recommended for web and print).
- `/png`: Raster formats with transparent backgrounds (For platforms that don't support SVG).
- `/social`: Pre-formatted banners and avatars for GitHub, Twitter/X, etc.

## 📐 Usage Guidelines

### ✅ Do's
- Use the provided SVG files whenever possible for razor-sharp rendering.
- Maintain sufficient clear space around the logomark (at least equal to the height of the inner icon).
- Use the logo to link back to [linkest.org](https://linkest.org) when representing affiliated projects.
- Ensure adequate contrast when placing the logo over a background.

### ❌ Don'ts
- **Do not alter the colors:** Do not recolor or change the gradient of the logomark.
- **Do not distort the shape:** Do not stretch, squash, or rotate the logomark.
- **Do not add effects:** Avoid adding drop shadows, outlines, or glow effects that are not in the original asset.
- **Do not use for unauthorized endorsement:** Do not use the logo to imply official partnership or endorsement without prior written consent from Linkest Foundation.
